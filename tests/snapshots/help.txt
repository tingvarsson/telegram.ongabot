Welcome traveler, my name is ONGAbot.
I'm the one and only, the truth speaker.

My duties are:
 - Uphold the law, obviously, with weekly ON/GA polls
 - Give praise to the faithful
 - Aid the needing
 - Condemn the wicked

Commandments:
/help - Get some aid in needing times
/changelog - Show recent changelog entries
/onga - This is the way, let me show you
/newevent [day=<date|weekday>] [time=<HH:MM>] [slots=<n>] [force=true] - Create a new event poll (defaults: wednesday, 18:30, 5 slots)
/cancelevent [<date|weekday>] - Cancel an active event
/updateevent [target_date=<date|weekday>] [day=<date|weekday>] [time=<HH:MM>] [slots=<n>] - Update an active event
/schedule [trigger_on=<weekday>] [day=<weekday>] [time=<HH:MM>] [slots=<n>] - Schedule weekly poll creation (defaults: sunday → wednesday, 18:30, 5 slots)
/reschedule [trigger_on=<weekday>] [day=<weekday>] [time=<HH:MM>] [slots=<n>] - Update the weekly schedule
/deschedule - Remove the weekly schedule
/authorize - Authorize this chat to use ONGAbot (bot admins only)
/deauthorize - Deauthorize this chat from using ONGAbot (bot admins only)
/statistics - Show all-time participation statistics for this chat
/leaderboard - Show the Banger Points leaderboard for this chat
/cs2 [<date|weekday>] - Show the CS2 results for a date
/cs2patches on|off - Post CS2 patch notes in this chat as Valve releases them
/topics - Show this chat's learned YouTube Short topic preferences
/linksteam <steam64|profile URL> - Link your Steam account so your CS2 games are recognised
/unlinksteam - Unlink your Steam account
